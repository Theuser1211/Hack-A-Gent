Built like a real 3D stack

✦

Renderer

Perspective projection, camera transforms and depth-aware point shading drawn frame by frame.

❆

Interaction

Drag to orbit with inertia, wheel to zoom, touch support and an idle auto-orbit that resumes on its own.

◎

Determinism

Every particle, ring and star is placed by a seeded generator — the same world renders every single time.

Worlds in this build

Particle Sphere

380 seeded particles on a fibonacci lattice with a geodesic edge mesh.

Orbital Rings

Two tilted rings sweeping around the core, split front/back for correct occlusion.

Parallax Starfield

A distant star shell that shifts with the camera for real depth.

Engine pipeline

  1. Seeded scene — fibonacci sphere, rings and star shell from a single seed.
  2. Camera — orbit yaw/pitch, zoom distance, eased target motion.
  3. Transform — spin rotation → camera space → perspective divide.
  4. Render — depth-shaded particles, edge mesh, core glow, parallax stars.
