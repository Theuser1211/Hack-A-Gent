How it works

1
Telemetry

A 24-hour residential profile (load, solar, price) at 15-minute resolution. Clearly labeled sample data.

2
Scenario engine

Your controls reshape the profile: EV block moves off-peak, HVAC trims in peak hours, the battery charges on solar surplus and discharges into the evening peak.

3
Cost model

Grid import × time-of-use tariff for every interval, summed across the day. Savings are a model estimate, not a hardware measurement.

4
Recommendations

Ranked actions with per-item estimated savings so the plan is actionable, not just pretty.
